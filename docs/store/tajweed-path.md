### Full description (≤ 4000)
<!-- en.full -->
Tajweed Path is a complete tajweed course in the recitation of Hafs from ʿAsim (by way of al-Shatibiyyah). You learn one rule at a time, on real ayat.

What's inside:
• 58 practical lessons in 10 units that follow the short surahs from al-Nas back to al-Duha, then al-Fatihah and beyond Juz ʿAmma.
• Every lesson has seven short steps: hear, notice, learn, say, find, check, then recite a passage that uses only rules you already know.
• Daily review in the sabaq, sabqi and manzil way: every rule you learn comes back at growing intervals until it sticks.
• Listen: ayat recited by al-Husary (teaching style), al-Husary and al-Minshawi, with the word being recited lit up, loop, slower speed, and a pause after each ayah for you to repeat.
• Record yourself and play it right after the reciter to compare. The app never grades your recitation; only a teacher can, and a teacher is optional.
• A card for every rule: what it is, Quran examples you can play, what it is easily confused with, the lines of Tuhfat al-Atfal and al-Jazariyyah that cover it, and its sources.
• Your progress: the rules you can recognise and the days you studied each week.
• Arabic and English, light and dark themes, larger text.

Your privacy:
• No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics or tracking.
• Your progress and recordings stay on your phone and are never uploaded. Back up to a file and restore it whenever you like.
• The Quran text is built into the app and works offline. Recitation audio streams from everyayah.com and needs a connection; you can save a whole unit's audio on your phone to listen offline.

Sources: Quran text from Tanzil, tajweed marks from the quran-tajweed project, word timings from quran-align, recitations from everyayah.com. Details and licences are on the Resources page in the app.

## App Store review notes (English)

No account is needed; every feature works signed out (there is no sign-in in this version). The microphone is used
only when the learner taps Record (a lesson's Say and Recite steps, a unit's finale, the Listen screen) to record
their own recitation and play it beside the reciter. Recordings stay on the device and are never uploaded. Recitation
audio streams from everyayah.com, and a unit's audio can be downloaded for offline use. The Quran text is bundled
(Tanzil). Backups are a JSON file the learner shares or saves through the system share sheet.

## Google Play: Data safety

Answers derived from the code (`mobile/lib`, `mobile/pubspec.yaml`, the merged release manifest), for the MVP
build with no account:

| Question | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? | **No** | Nothing the learner enters or records is sent off the device. The app has no backend calls, analytics, crash reporting or ads SDK (dependencies: just_audio, audio_session, record, file_picker, share_plus, url_launcher, path_provider, intl, hijri). |
| Audio (voice or sound recordings) | Not collected | Recordings are `.m4a` files in the app's own storage; they are processed on the device only and are excluded from Android Auto Backup (`res/xml/*_rules.xml`). |
| App activity (progress, answers) | Not collected | Stored in the app's own storage (`userdata.json`). Android Auto Backup may copy it to the user's own Google backup; that is the OS's backup, not collection by the developer. |
| Files and docs | Not collected | A backup file leaves the device only when the learner picks a destination in the share sheet (user-initiated). Restore reads a file the learner picks. |
| Device or other IDs, location, contacts, personal info | Not collected | None of these APIs are used. |
| Network requests | — | Audio files are fetched from everyayah.com over HTTPS (a plain file request; no identifiers are added). Links (YouTube, quran.com, Shamela, credits) open in the browser or the YouTube app. |
| Is all user data encrypted in transit? | Yes (no user data is transmitted; the audio requests are HTTPS) | |
| Do you provide a way for users to request that their data be deleted? | Not applicable (no data collected). In the app: Settings › Reset everything, and Delete all recordings. Uninstalling removes everything. | |

Permissions in the release manifest: `INTERNET` (audio), `RECORD_AUDIO` (asked at the first Record tap, after the
app's own explanation), `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` (added by ExoPlayer, normal permission).
