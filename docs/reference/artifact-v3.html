Seven principles

  • التلقي والمشافهةLearn from a teacherTajweed passes from mouth to ear. Books and videos explain; a qualified teacher hears what you can't. One session a week is enough to start.
  • الجلي قبل الخفيClear errors firstFix اللحن الجلي (a wrong letter or vowel, which can change meaning) before اللحن الخفي (fine details of the rules).
  • الأداء قبل المصطلحSound before namesSaying ع and ح correctly matters more than knowing their names. Theory serves your tongue.
  • قارئ قدوةOne model reciterCopy one teaching recitation, المصحف المعلم للحصري, until it becomes your natural way of reading.
  • سجّل واستمعRecord yourselfRecord, then compare with the model. You will catch most of your own mistakes.
  • قليل دائمDaily and smallForty minutes a day beats one long session a week. Tajweed is a skill of the tongue and needs repetition.
  • طبّق فورًاApply on real versesEvery rule you learn, find it in جزء عمّ the same day. Keep a mistakes log and check it weekly.
