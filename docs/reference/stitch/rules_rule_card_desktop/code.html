Al-Ikhfā' al-Ḥaqīqī

الإخفاء الحقيقي
The True Concealment — Pronouncing the Nūn or Tanwīn between manifestation and assimilation.
translate Linguistic Meaning
As-Satr (الستر) الستر والإخفاء عن الأعين

Literally signifies hiding, covering, or veiling something from sensory perception.

menu_book Tajweed Definition

Articulation between Iẓhār and Idghām, devoid of any shaddah, while retaining the complete Ghunnah (nasalization) of 2 counts.

Tafkhīm vs. Tarqīq of the Ghunnah

مراتب الغنة: تفخيماً وترقيقاً

Unlike the Ghunnah of Idghām (which remains inherently light), the Ghunnah of Ikhfā' al-Ḥaqīqī follows the heaviness or lightness of the subsequent letter.

Tafkhīm (Heavy Ghunnah) When followed by (ص، ض، ط، ظ، ق)
Tarqīq (Light Ghunnah) When followed by the remaining 10 letters
