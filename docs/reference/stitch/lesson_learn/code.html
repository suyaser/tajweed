1 What happens
المفهوم الأكاديمي

When a Nūn Sākinah (نْ) or Tanween is followed by any of the 15 letters of Ikhfāʾ, the ‘N’ sound is lightly concealed at the point of articulation with a nasal resonance (Ghunnah) without total tongue closure.

حروف الإخفاء الـ ١٥

Equivalent to holding the vocal stream steadily for the duration of opening or closing a finger without rushing (~1.5–2 seconds).

4 When it applies
شروط الانطباق
1. Within a single word Occurs mid-word directly before an Ikhfāʾ letter.
أَنفُسَهُمْ مَنثُورًا
2. Between two words Nūn or Tanween at end of word, letter starts the next.
مِن شَرِّ مَن ذَا الَّذِي
5 Practical Example
المثال العملي

« مِن شَــرِّ مَا خَلَقَ »

Scholarly Guidance تنبيه وتوجيه

Mufakhkham vs. Tarqeeq: If the following letter is heavy (ص، ض، ط، ظ، ق), the Ghunnah adopts heavy resonance. If the letter is light, the Ghunnah remains soft and thin.
