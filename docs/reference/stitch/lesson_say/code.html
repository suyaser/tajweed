What to listen for (ضابط الإتقان):

Ensure your tongue does not press firmly against the gum-line of the upper incisors. The Nūn sound should be partially masked, allowing the غنة (resonant nasal hum) to flow softly for two measured beats.

How did your Ghunnah sound?

التقييم الذاتي
auto_stories Rule Reference: Ikhfa Haqiqi

Literally means "concealment". In Tajweed, it is pronouncing a non-voweled Nūn or Tanween between clear Izhar and full Idgham without Tashdeed, keeping the nasal Ghunnah on the following 15 letters.
