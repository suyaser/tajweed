# Tajweed competitors: how Tajweed Path compares

Written 2026-11-20. This doc compares Tajweed Path (specs 09 and 10) with tajweed and Quran-recitation products. General language-app UX (Duolingo, Busuu, ELSA) is in doc 11 and is not repeated here. Sources are numbered [n] at the end.

**How much to trust this doc.** Most evidence comes from App Store listings, vendor pages and a few reviews. I did not install the apps or look at their screenshots. That makes the "feel" column the weakest one. Where a claim rests only on vendor marketing, it says "claims". Where I could not check something, it says **unverified**.

**Candidates dropped after checking:**
- **Bayyinah TV:** no tajweed course turned up. Bayyinah is tafsir and Arabic [search, no hit].
- **Zad Academy:** a free 2-year programme with seven subjects, and tajweed is not one of them [25].
- **SeekersGuidance:** no tajweed course was found.
- **"Mishkah":** no app by that name was found. The nearest is Mishkat, a colour-coded reader with no course.
- **Huda Academy tajweed:** its listing page returned 404.

## 3. Where we are clearly ahead

1. **We are the only product that is both a course and ordered by surah.** Every app competitor (C, D, G, J) teaches rule by rule, Qaida first. Every surah-based source (M, N) is passive video. We combine Alaa Hamed's order with exercises.
2. **The closing passage verified by script.** No competitor promises that the passage you recite at the end of a lesson contains only rules you have learned. This is unique and hard to copy without per-word tajweed data and a build test.
3. **Find, checked against tajweed data.** Learn Quran Tajwid has theory tests. Tajweeed has quizzes. None makes you locate the rule in a real ayah and checks it.
4. **Spaced review in tajweed.** Learn Quran Tajwid, Tajweeed, Udemy and the courses have no spaced review that I could find. Tarteel tracks memorisation mistakes, not rules. Quranic has spaced repetition, but for vocabulary [10].
5. **Honesty about feedback.** We do not ship a score we cannot back. Tarteel's own listing admits it does not correct tajweed [1]. The best open research model reaches a tajweed F1 of 75.8% on only ghunnah, qalqalah and madd, on a 159-sample benchmark [24]. One wrong verdict in four is not a teacher. Al-Dirassa draws the same line [17].
6. **Price and pressure.** Free, no ads, no paywalled results (Learn Quran Tajwid's top complaint [7]), and no streak or leaderboard.
7. **Rule depth for a self-study app.** Rawm and ishmām, the sakt points, imālah, tashīl, al-ḍād and al-ẓāʾ, marātib al-tafkhīm, plus the mutun. Only the academies (K, N) go deeper.

## 6. What not to copy, and why

1. **Scores and grades for recitation** (Tajweeed's A–D pitch grade, TajweedMate's "smart analysis", Dugsiya's red letters). Pitch is not tajweed. The best published model is wrong on about a quarter of rule judgments for 3 rules [24]. Tarteel, the best-funded team, still doesn't claim tajweed [1][2], and its users report false flags [1]. A confident wrong grade teaches the learner to satisfy a model, not to recite correctly. This breaks our honesty principle.
2. **Streaks, daily-goal pressure and leaderboards** (Tarteel, Tajweeed [1][14]). For worship, public ranking invites riyāʾ, and streaks punish illness and menstruation (doc 11 §1.5). Private circles with "days studied" are enough.
3. **Paywalls on feedback the learner has already earned.** Learn Quran Tajwid hides test results behind Pro, and it is the most-cited complaint [7]. Tarteel puts its core feature behind $240 a year [1].
4. **Selling or awarding a "diploma" or certificate** (IU Madinah sells a $39 "Quran Diploma" [19]). Only a teacher with a sanad can grant an ijazah. Any badge we issue for finishing the course must say "course completed", never anything that reads like ijāzah or "certified".
5. **Qaida-first, rule-by-rule order** (C, D, G, J). It works for children and beginners, but it is the opposite of our practice-first, surah-based design, and our learner already reads. Point to a Qaida instead of becoming one.
6. **Long lecture video as the core** (Get Quranic's 25 hours, Udemy). Video should support practice, not replace it. Link to it, don't host it.
7. **Live-tutor marketplace** (Qutor, maqāriʾ). That is a different business with vetting, payments and safeguarding. Link to the ones that already exist.
8. **Ten qirāʾāt now.** It multiplies the content review work. Hafs covers almost all of our audience.
