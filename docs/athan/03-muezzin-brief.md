# Muezzin recording brief (Muqim · مُقيم)

What to give a muezzin we commission. One page; the agreement terms are at the end.

## 1. What to record

Three voices, two athans each:

| # | Voice | Recording | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Makkah (Haramain) style** | Regular athan | Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha |
| A2 | Makkah style | **Fajr athan** (with الصلاة خير من النوم) | Fajr |
| B1 | **Egyptian style** | Regular athan | Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha |
| B2 | Egyptian style | Fajr athan | Fajr |
| C1 | **Madinah (Masjid an-Nabawi) style**: slower, longer phrases, softer ornamentation than Makkah | Regular athan | Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha |
| C2 | Madinah style | Fajr athan | Fajr |

Optional, if the session allows: the iqamah in the same voice (for a later feature).

The voices can come from one muezzin who does several styles well, or from different muezzins. If the budget covers only two styles, record Makkah and Madinah first (the two users ask for most) and Egyptian later. The maqam is the muezzin's choice; we want the familiar character of each style, not an imitation of a named sheikh.

## 2. Text

The common athan, opening with four takbirs:

اللهُ أكبرُ اللهُ أكبرُ، اللهُ أكبرُ اللهُ أكبرُ
أشهدُ أن لا إلهَ إلا الله ×2
أشهدُ أنَّ محمدًا رسولُ الله ×2
حيَّ على الصلاة ×2
حيَّ على الفلاح ×2
**(Fajr only)** الصلاةُ خيرٌ من النوم ×2
اللهُ أكبرُ اللهُ أكبرُ
لا إلهَ إلا الله

Please avoid the known errors (لحن جلي in the athan): no madd on the first hamza of «الله» («آلله»), no lengthening of the ba in «أكبر» («أكبار»), «حيَّ» with its shaddah, and a clear ha in «الصلاة» and «الفلاح». Our scholar will listen before release.

## 3. How to record

- **Room:** quiet and dead (a carpeted room, wardrobe or booth). No fan, AC, traffic or echo. No loudspeaker, no added reverb or effects. We add a light reverb ourselves if wanted.
- **Microphone:** one decent mic about 20–30 cm away, slightly off-axis. A good phone in a quiet room is acceptable if nothing better is available.
- **Level:** loudest peaks around −6 dBFS. Nothing clipped or distorted.
- **Format:** WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit, mono, unedited. Please also send any edited version you prefer.
- **Takes:** 2–3 full takes of each recording.
- **Silence:** 2 seconds of silence before the first takbir and after the last word.
- **Pauses:** a natural, clear breath between phrases. We cut the iOS alert from the first two takbir phrases, which must fit in under 30 seconds. About 20–28 seconds for both is ideal.
- **Length:** each athan about 2½–4 minutes.

## 4. Agreement (in writing, before payment)

- The muezzin confirms the recordings are his own performance, made for us, and that he has the right to grant this licence.
- **Licence:** perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable and non-exclusive. We may use, edit, cut, convert and distribute the recordings in the Muqim and Tajweed Path apps, their websites and store listings, and future apps of ours. This includes delivery through Apple and Google.
- **Credit:** his name as he wants it shown (Arabic and English) in the app's About page. He may choose to stay anonymous.
- **Fee** and payment date, or that the recording is given free (waqf / fī sabīlillāh). Either way, the licence terms above still apply.
- His contact details, so we can ask for retakes or later items such as the iqamah.

## 5. Delivery

A shared folder with one file per take, named like `A2_makkah_fajr_take2.wav`, plus the signed agreement (a PDF or a photo of it). We keep the agreement with the app's licence records (`docs/licences/`).
